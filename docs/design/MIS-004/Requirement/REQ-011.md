# Requirement: REQ-011 Brain Style Graph Navigation

The editor SHALL provide a central mind-map / TheBrain style view with the focused card centered. The view SHALL show the parent (from the chosen shortest-path chain), and MAY show siblings (same card_type) linked from that parent even if they are not linked to the focused card.
