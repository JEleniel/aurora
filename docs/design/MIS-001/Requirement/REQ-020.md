# Requirement: REQ-020 Boundary and Note Semantics

Boundary cards SHALL be used to group related cards for rendering and navigation; they may have a subtype such as End to limit recursion. Note cards SHALL be used only as annotative leaves (incoming links only) and SHALL NOT introduce new structural model elements.
