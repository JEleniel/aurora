# Requirement: REQ-025 Model Integrity Validation

Aurora models SHALL be machine-validatable for integrity: every non-Mission, non-Note card SHALL have at least one incoming link; every card SHALL be reachable from the Mission; and all links[].target values SHALL reference an existing card id.
